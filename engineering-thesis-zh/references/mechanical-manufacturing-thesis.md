# Mechanical And Manufacturing Thesis Pattern

Use this reference for theses about mechanical equipment, intelligent manufacturing, assembly lines, process planning, maintenance, or production-system applications.

## Common Chapter Pattern

1. 绪论: industrial scenario, equipment/process problem, research significance, status review.
2. 对象与需求分析: production object, process route, equipment state, maintenance or quality requirements.
3. 模型与方法: process model, equipment degradation model, scheduling/maintenance strategy, evaluation system.
4. 系统或实验平台设计: simulation architecture, data flow, digital twin mapping, key modules.
5. 实验与结果分析: scenario design, comparison experiments, sensitivity analysis, engineering interpretation.
6. 总结与展望.

## Evidence Rules

- Distinguish real production data from simulated assumptions.
- Do not imply factory deployment unless there is field evidence.
- Maintenance policies must match implemented policy names and trigger rules.
- Figures should show process routes, equipment state transitions, simulation flow, or digital-twin mapping.

## Writing Style

- Anchor claims in the manufacturing object: station, process, equipment, order, operation, health state.
- Explain why metrics matter operationally: makespan, flow time, tardiness, utilization, downtime, maintenance cost.
- Keep engineering value conservative: "为后续现场参数接入提供基础" is safer than "实现工业级优化".
