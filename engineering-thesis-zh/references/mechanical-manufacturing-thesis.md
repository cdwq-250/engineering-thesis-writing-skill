# Mechanical And Manufacturing Thesis Pattern

Use this reference for theses about mechanical equipment, intelligent manufacturing, assembly lines, process planning, maintenance, or production-system applications.

## Common Chapter Pattern

1. 绪论：工业场景、设备或工艺问题、研究意义、国内外现状。
2. 对象与需求分析：生产对象、工艺路线、设备状态、维护或质量需求。
3. 模型与方法：工艺模型、设备退化模型、调度或维护策略、评价体系。
4. 系统或实验平台设计：仿真架构、数据流、数字孪生映射、关键模块。
5. 实验与结果分析：场景设计、对比实验、敏感性分析、工程解释。
6. 总结与展望。

## Evidence Rules

- Distinguish real production data from simulated assumptions.
- Do not imply factory deployment unless there is field evidence.
- Maintenance policies must match implemented policy names, trigger rules, and parameter settings.
- Figures should show process routes, equipment state transitions, simulation flow, or digital-twin mapping.
- Quality, efficiency, and maintenance claims must be tied to measurable indicators such as makespan, flow time, tardiness, utilization, downtime, maintenance cost, or defect rate.

## Writing Style

- Anchor claims in the manufacturing object: station, process, equipment, order, operation, health state.
- Explain why metrics matter operationally.
- Use conservative engineering value statements, such as “为后续现场参数接入提供基础”， instead of unsupported claims such as “实现工业级优化”。
